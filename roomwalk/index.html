How a Lumenstep tour works

  1. 1

    Look around

    Drag (or use a mouse) to pan. On a keyboard, WASD walks you through the connected rooms.

  2. 2

    Step through doorways

    Glowing hotspots mark every opening. Tap one — or the room chips — to cross the threshold.

  3. 3

    Read the house

    Info pins call out materials and moments, the way a listing tour would on a walk-through.

Four rooms, one path

Living room

A plaster hearth, linen sofa, and south light. The house’s gathering room — doors to kitchen and bedroom.

Kitchen

Sage cabinets, a maple island, and a garden window. Pass through to the bath or back to the parlor.

Bedroom

A linen bed against the west wall, a reading lamp, and a quiet corner looking toward the living room.

Bathroom

Subway tile, a built-in tub, brass-toned fixtures. The last stop on the Willow Hall loop.
