Finding your way

  • Look — drag on the screen, or move the mouse after clicking the view.
  • Walk — WASD or arrow keys on a keyboard; use the stick on a phone.
  • Rooms — tap glowing doorway labels, the floor plan, or the chips below.
  • Pins — the dotted markers open a short note about what’s in the room.
